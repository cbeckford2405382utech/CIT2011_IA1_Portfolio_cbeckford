My Favourite AI Business Idea

I like the idea of an AI Study Assistant that helps students learn by explaining topics in simple steps, generating practice questions, and tracking weak areas so the student improves faster.

How it can be applied in my world

As a BSc Computing student, this tool could help me review concepts, practice exam questions, and strengthen understanding before tests and assignments.

Ethical Issues

  • Academic honesty: AI must not encourage cheating or writing full assignments for students.
  • Privacy: Student data must be protected and not shared without consent.
  • Bias: The AI should treat all users fairly and avoid harmful stereotypes.

My solution to moral concerns

The AI should focus on tutoring and learning support, not completing graded work. It should include privacy controls, and it should clearly show sources where needed.
