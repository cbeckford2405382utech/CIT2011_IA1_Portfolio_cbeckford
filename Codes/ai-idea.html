My Favourite AI Business Idea

I would choose an AI-powered Student Study & Assignment Planner for university students. The system helps students plan deadlines, break work into steps, and keep consistent study routines.

How it applies to my world

  • Turns big assignments into daily tasks (so no last-minute panic).
  • Helps track progress across multiple courses.
  • Suggests revision schedules based on upcoming tests.

Why it would be useful

Many students struggle because they don’t manage time well, not because they’re unintelligent. AI could support planning, reminders, and structured learning.

Ethical Issues + My Solutions

  • Privacy: Student schedules and performance are sensitive.
  • Over-reliance: Students may depend on AI instead of building discipline.
  • Bias: Bad data could push unfair recommendations.

My solution: keep data minimal, allow users to delete data anytime, and clearly show how recommendations are made. The AI should guide planning, not replace personal responsibility.

Example App in This Area

Example: Todoist (task planning). My AI version would add smart study breakdowns and academic scheduling features.
