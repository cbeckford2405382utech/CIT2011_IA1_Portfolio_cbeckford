AI Business Idea: Smart Study Coach for University Students

My favourite AI business idea is an AI-powered “Smart Study Coach” that helps students manage workload, understand topics, and create structured study plans. The AI would analyse the student’s schedule, deadlines, and learning goals, then recommend daily tasks and explain difficult concepts in simpler terms.

How It Applies to My World

As a computing student, I juggle multiple modules and deadlines. This tool would reduce confusion by organising what to do each day and helping me revise efficiently.

Why It Would Be Useful

It saves time, improves consistency, and supports students who struggle with planning or understanding topics. It could also provide quick practice questions and feedback.

Ethical Issues + My Solution

Ethical concerns include privacy (student data), over-reliance on AI, and academic honesty. My solution: strict privacy settings, clear warnings, and a “learning-first” mode that explains steps instead of giving shortcuts.
