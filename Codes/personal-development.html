Chapter 4 — Achieve Your Goals

This chapter focuses on setting clear goals and committing to them daily. Goals give direction and keep you from drifting. A key message is that you must decide what you want, write it down, and take consistent action.

How I apply it: I break my semester into weekly targets (labs, assignments, study hours), so I’m always moving forward instead of rushing at the end.

Chapter 5 — The Proper Use of Time

Time is a resource you can’t get back. The chapter teaches planning, prioritizing important tasks, and avoiding distractions. Small wasted time daily becomes big lost progress over months.

How I apply it: I time-block my evenings for coding, then review notes after. If I don’t plan, the day plans me.

Chapter 6 — Surround Yourself with the Best People

The chapter explains that your environment and the people around you influence your habits, mindset, and standards. Being around serious, supportive people helps you level up.

How I apply it: I try to work with classmates who actually want to pass and improve. I also take feedback seriously because it helps me grow faster.

Chapter 7 — Be a Lifelong Learner

This chapter teaches that learning should never stop. Reading, studying, practicing skills, and staying curious improves your value and keeps you prepared for opportunities.

How I apply it: I keep building small projects and improving my portfolio. I want to be the kind of engineer who can learn new technologies quickly and still deliver quality work.
